# Support HI-UEO

HI-UEO is an AI-powered platform being developed to help Indonesian small businesses and entrepreneurs access practical AI tools and automation.

Your sponsorship helps keep the project moving forward: improving reliability, strengthening security, developing useful features, and making practical AI more accessible to UMKM.

## Why your support matters

HI-UEO is built with a long-term goal: make useful AI capabilities easier to access for small businesses that may not have large technology budgets. Sponsorship helps fund the ongoing work behind that goal.

## How sponsorship funds may be used

- Platform development and maintenance
- Reliability, security, and testing
- Improvements to the user experience
- Research and development of useful AI features
- Infrastructure and operational costs
